Season 2 field guide

The armoury

Twelve legendary weapons, and only one of each exists. Here's what every one of them does, how you get one, and what happens once you're holding it.

One of eachEvery legendary is unique. Whoever holds it is announced to the whole server.
Won at RiftsLegendaries can't be crafted. They appear at a Rift every day at 7:30 PM server time.
Hunted while heldCarry one and your location gets broadcast while a bounty grows on you.
Fed by killsA weapon that goes days without a player kill weakens, then leaves you.

Using a legendary

Abilities, tiers and awakening

Using abilities

  • Hold the weapon and type /ability1 or /ability2. Bind them to keys with a keybind or macro mod so you can use them mid-fight.
  • Bows, crossbows and the trident still work normally on right-click.
  • Every ability has its own cooldown, shown on your action bar if you try too early.
  • Each use also adds Strain.

Awakening (Tiers)

  • Every weapon starts at Tier I, reaches Tier II at 5 player kills and Tier III at 10.
  • Kills are stored on the weapon, so its power goes with it when it changes hands. Taking a Tier III weapon off someone is a huge prize.
  • A kill counts for every legendary you're carrying.
  • No farming: killing the same player again within an hour gives no awakening progress (it still feeds the weapon).
  • Carrying the Dragon Egg puts all your weapons at Tier III while you hold it.

Indestructible

  • Legendaries never break, never despawn, and survive fire, lava, cactus and explosions.
  • They can't be enchanted, renamed or disenchanted.
  • If one falls into the void it goes back to its last holder. Mobs and hoppers can't pick them up.

Finding weapons

  • /whohas [weapon] shows who holds each weapon, or which are still waiting in the Rifts.
  • /whereis [weapon] finds a weapon lying on the ground.
  • Whenever a weapon changes hands, the whole server is told.
How you get one

Rifts

Legendaries have no recipes. They manifest at Rifts, and every weapon starts sealed away, so all twelve have to come out of a Rift. Type /rift to see when the next one opens and how many legendaries are left.

  1. WarningChat warnings 1 hour, 15 minutes and 5 minutes before a Rift opens. A Rift opens every day at 7:30 PM server time.
  2. It opensCoordinates go out in chat, on screen and on a boss bar counting down 10 minutes.
  3. Safe zoneNo PvP within 30 blocks of the Rift during the countdown. Get in position.
  4. It landsLightning strikes, the weapon drops sealed, Rift Wardens spawn and PvP is back on.
  5. Claim itKill the Wardens to break the seal. The first player to pick it up keeps it.
  • Rift Wardens: two Iron Golems and two Wither Skeletons with Strength III. They go straight for the nearest player and drop nothing.
  • The seal also breaks on its own after 3 minutes, and the Wardens vanish after 5.
  • Rifts never open inside land claims.
  • Grand Rifts: now and then an admin makes a Rift a Grand Rift. A player, the Host, guards the weapon instead of the Wardens, and the Host's bounty is raised so everyone knows where to find them. Slay the Host to break the seal. Their exact location is announced every 5 minutes, and when they fall, every legendary they were carrying scatters into a Rift of its own somewhere in the world.

Once you hold one

The Hunt

Carrying a legendary in your inventory makes you Hunted. The longer you hold it, the bigger the reward for whoever takes you down, and the easier you are to find.

  • Every 10 minutes, the server announces roughly where each holder is.
  • Your bounty gains a ★ for every 30 minutes you're online carrying a legendary, up to 10★.
  • Whoever kills you claims it: 2 diamonds and 2 XP levels per ★, plus whatever you drop.
  • Your bounty resets when you die or stop carrying a legendary. Stashing it in your ender chest hides you, but then it can't be fed (see Hunger).
  • /hunt shows everyone currently Hunted, their stars, and where they were last sensed.

Keep it fed

Hunger

Legendaries hunger for kills. Any player kill while you're carrying one feeds it. Hiding a weapon in a chest doesn't stop the clock: it keeps starving, and will already have starved by the time you take it out.

Days without a killWhat happens
0 to 3Sated. All good.
3It loses a tier.
6It loses another tier (it never drops below Tier I).
9Restless. While you carry it you have Hunger II and Weakness I, and it whispers to you in chat.
12It abandons you: it vanishes, the server is told, and it goes back to the Rifts.
  • One kill resets it to Sated, and any lost tiers come straight back.
  • Picking up a weapon from someone else always gives you at least 24 hours before it can lose a tier.
  • You get a warning 12 hours before each step, and the weapon's tooltip shows its Hunger at all times.
Don't spam

Strain

Every ability use adds Strain (0 to 100), shown next to each ability above. It drains by 1 per second once you've gone 3 seconds without using an ability. Lean on your abilities too hard and they'll hurt you.

040: Slowness I60: + Weakness I80: + Mining Fatigue II

At 100 you Overload: Wither II for 4 seconds and your weapon's abilities are locked for 8 seconds. Overloading in the middle of a fight can kill you.

Combat

  • Hitting or being hit puts you in combat for 30 seconds. Logging out in combat gets you a warning, and doing it repeatedly can earn a temporary ban.
  • In combat, elytras don't work and ender pearls have a 15-second cooldown.
  • In combat you can riptide at most twice every 20 seconds.

Maces and spears

  • Normal maces can be crafted but not enchanted, and their smash attack works once every 40 seconds.
  • Lunge spears get 3 lunges, then a 20-second cooldown. Unused lunges refresh after 20 seconds without lunging.
  • Golden apples are cheaper: 4 gold ingots in a plus around an apple.
